<section class="content-card card about" id="about-me">
  <h2 class="section-label">About Me</h2>
  <p>Hi there! I completed an MRes with Distinction in Medical Robotics and Image-Guided Intervention at the <a href="https://www.imperial.ac.uk/hamlyn-centre/">Hamlyn Centre</a>, Department of Medicine, <strong>Imperial College London</strong>, in September 2026. My research interests include <strong>Computer Vision</strong> and <strong>Medical Imaging</strong> in robotic-assisted surgery. I was supervised by <a href="https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/d.zhang17">Dr. Dandan Zhang</a> and <a href="https://qingbiaoli.github.io/">Dr. Qingbiao Li</a>.</p>
  <p>In July 2025, I completed my BSc in Information and Computing Science at <a href="https://www.xjtlu.edu.cn/en">Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University (XJTLU)</a> with First Class Honours, including a one-year research exchange in Computer Science at <a href="https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/">the University of Liverpool</a>. During my undergraduate studies, I conducted research in Human–Computer Interaction and Mobile Data Visualization under the supervision of <a href="https://scholar.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/persons/YuLiu02/">Dr. Yu Liu</a>.</p>
  <p>My recent work focuses on surgical scene understanding and semantic segmentation for robot-assisted surgery, with an emphasis on robust performance in real endoscopic scenes and on label-efficient pipelines that turn pixel-level understanding into actionable decision support.</p>
</section>
